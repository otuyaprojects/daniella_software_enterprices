FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Questions before we build?

What type of software do you build? +

We build custom web applications, business systems, SaaS platforms, dashboards, APIs, management platforms and digital products.

Can you build an entire product from scratch? +

Yes. A project can begin with an idea or business problem and progress through discovery, design, engineering, testing and deployment.

Do you work with existing systems? +

Existing applications can be reviewed, improved, integrated, redesigned or extended depending on the requirements.

Can you build software for Nigerian businesses? +

Yes. Systems can be designed around local business workflows, operational requirements, currencies and organizational structures.

How does a project begin? +

The process begins with a conversation about the problem, objectives, users and desired outcome. From there, the appropriate scope and development approach can be defined.

Do you provide maintenance after launch? +

Ongoing maintenance, improvements, monitoring and additional development can be part of the long-term product relationship.
